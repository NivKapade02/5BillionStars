Account and reporting

Only your designated Firebase admin can read guest details, manage bookings, record payments and save follow-up notes.

Tracking records page views, form openings, successful saves, WhatsApp and phone-link clicks, and visible-session heartbeats every two minutes. Admin routes and browsers that disable tracking are excluded. Browser blocking or interrupted connections can leave gaps.

The data window is limited to protect Spark usage. This dashboard does not automatically verify bank payments, WhatsApp delivery or exact visitor location.
