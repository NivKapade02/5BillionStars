Before you book

Are the prices per person? Are meals and taxes included?

Yes. Tent Stay is ₹{{ pricing.tent.offer }} per person, reduced from ₹{{ pricing.tent.regular }}. Cottage Stay is ₹{{ pricing.cottage.offer }} per person, reduced from ₹{{ pricing.cottage.regular }}. All meals and taxes are included. These are per-person prices, not the cost of an entire tent or cottage. Confirm availability and your group's final total with our team.

How do I confirm my booking and pay the advance?

Submitting the form saves an availability request. Tap Send in WhatsApp, agree on availability and the total with our team, then pay {{ terms.advancePercent }}% in advance using Google Pay or PhonePe on {{ terms.paymentNumber }}. Share your payment reference and receive confirmation from our team. Submitting a form alone is not a reservation.

What does a child's stay cost?

Children under 5 stay free. Children aged 5–10 are charged ₹{{ terms.childRate }} per child. Guests above 10 pay the applicable tent or cottage per-person rate. Include all children in your guest count and share their ages with the team.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Same-day cancellations are not refundable. For earlier cancellations, ask our team to confirm the applicable deduction and exact refundable amount in writing before you pay. Discuss rescheduling and weather-related arrangements with our team.

What facilities are available?

The campsite has 11 clean and hygienic washrooms, hot water, fans inside the cottages and ample parking. Contact our team about any accessibility needs or specific washroom arrangements before travelling.

How do we reach the campsite?

The campsite approach is a 100-metre cement road from Paud–Lonavala Road. Use the Google Map in the contact section and ask our team for arrival guidance.

What meals and activities are planned?

Your stay includes all meals: evening tea and snacks, BBQ, dinner, and morning tea with poha. The schedule also lists games and evening entertainment. Share your vegetarian or non-vegetarian preferences with the team before booking.

When do we arrive and leave?

The schedule begins at 4:30 PM, with checkout at 11:00 AM the next day. Confirm arrangements with the team if you plan to arrive earlier or later.

What happens to my booking information?

The details entered in the availability form are saved in the campsite's Google booking sheet and included in your prepared WhatsApp message. Contact the campsite at +91 8624908808 with questions about your booking information.
