What is the full price, including meals and charges?

Tent Stay is ₹{{ pricing.tent.offer }}/- and Cottage Stay is ₹{{ pricing.cottage.offer }}/- per adult per night, including all meals and taxes. A separate ₹{{ terms.networkHandlingRate }} handling charge per adult per night makes the totals ₹{{ adultTotals.tent }} and ₹{{ adultTotals.cottage }} respectively. These are per-adult prices, not the cost of an entire tent or cottage. Children under 5 stay free; ages 5–10 pay ₹{{ terms.childRate }} per child per night. Confirm availability and your group's final total with our team before paying.

How do I confirm my booking and pay the advance?

The form prepares a WhatsApp message with your details and price estimate. Open it and tap Send. Our team confirms availability, the final quote and terms in WhatsApp before you pay {{ terms.advancePercent }}% in advance using Google Pay or PhonePe on {{ terms.paymentNumber }}. Send your payment screenshot directly in the same WhatsApp chat. The team checks the bank transaction before confirming your stay. Preparing a form alone is not a reservation.

What does a child's stay cost?

Children under 5 stay free. Children aged 5–10 are charged ₹{{ terms.childRate }} per child per night. Guests above 10 pay the applicable tent or cottage per-person rate. Include all children in your guest count and share their ages with the team.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Cancellation deductions apply to the advance paid and are measured before 4:00 PM India time on the check-in date: 72+ hours: 5%; 48 to under 72 hours: 7%; 24 to under 48 hours: 10%; under 24 hours: no refund. Approved refunds are initiated within seven working days. One reschedule is allowed at least 72 hours before check-in, subject to availability and price differences; later changes follow cancellation rules. Campsite-initiated cancellations receive a full advance refund.

Are toilets attached to the cottages?

Toilets are located beside the cottages, not attached inside. Hot water, fans in the cottages and parking are available. Contact our team about any accessibility needs or specific washroom arrangements before travelling.

Are boating and kayaking included?

No. Boating and kayaking are nearby activities offered by other operators and charged separately. Our campsite does not provide these activities. Contact the activity operator about availability and prices.

How do we reach the campsite?

The campsite approach is a 100-metre cement road from Paud–Lonavala Road. Use the Google Map in the contact section and ask our team for arrival guidance.

What meals and activities are planned?

Your stay includes all meals: evening tea and snacks, BBQ, dinner, and morning tea with poha. The schedule also lists games and evening entertainment. Share your vegetarian or non-vegetarian preferences with the team before booking.

When do we arrive and leave?

The schedule begins at 4:00 PM, with checkout at 11:00 AM the next day. Confirm arrangements with the team if you plan to arrive earlier or later.

What happens to my booking information?

Submitting the form saves your contact information, stay details, guest and meal counts, comments and price estimate for our team in the private admin dashboard. Payment receipts are shared directly in WhatsApp. WhatsApp receives only the information and payment screenshots you choose to send. Our team manages your request in WhatsApp. Contact +91 8624908808 for questions or requests to correct or delete information held by the team.
