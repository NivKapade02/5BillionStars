About Us

5 Billion Stars

5 Billion Stars Camping & Agro Tourism offers tent and cottage stays overlooking Pawana Lake, near Tikona Fort.

Browse real campsite photographs, see the overnight schedule, and choose a stay for your group. Tent accommodation is for 2–3 people; cottage accommodation is for 4–5 people. Prices are per person.

Our team welcomes you to a campsite with 11 clean and hygienic washrooms, hot water, fans in the cottages and ample parking. The approach is a 100-metre cement road from Paud–Lonavala Road.

All meals and taxes are included in your stay price. Confirm your dates and group total with our team, pay a 40% advance, and receive your booking confirmation.
