About Us

5 Billion Stars

5 Billion Stars Camping & Agro Tourism is a campsite overlooking Pawana Lake, also known as Pawna Lake, with Sahyadri mountain, sunset and fort views. Enjoy lake-view tent and A-frame cottage stays near Tikona Fort in Pune district, Maharashtra. Plan a camping getaway from Pune, Mumbai or Lonavala.

Browse real campsite photographs, see the overnight schedule, and choose a stay for your group. Tent accommodation is for 2–3 people; cottage accommodation is for 4–5 people. Prices are per person.

Toilets are located beside the cottages, not attached inside. Hot water, fans in the cottages and ample parking are available. The approach is a 100-metre cement road from Paud–Lonavala Road.

All meals and taxes are included in your stay price. Confirm your dates and group total with our team, pay a 40% advance, and receive your booking confirmation.
