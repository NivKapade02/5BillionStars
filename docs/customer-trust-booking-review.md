Reviewed on 16 November 2026 from the perspective of a first-time guest choosing
a Pawana Lake stay. Changes are local, not deployed. This is a conversion review,
not a claim of measured booking uplift.

## Main findings

The site already has useful campsite photography, accommodation details, meal
information, a schedule, real contact details, Google Maps links and a booking
request flow. Its largest trust gaps were inconsistent price presentation,
unclear toilet arrangements, late social proof, and wording that could make a
guest confuse an availability request with a confirmed stay.

## Pricing integrity

The existing prices and calculation rules were preserved. The displayed adult
totals now include the existing ₹1 handling charge. Tent base rate remains ₹999;
cottage base rate remains ₹1,599. Under 5 remains free; ages 5–10 remain ₹700 per
child per night. The advance remains 40% after availability and final quote are
agreed with the team.

For example, two adults in a tent for one night total ₹2,000, including handling;
the 40% advance is ₹800 and the remaining balance is ₹1,200. This is an estimate,
not an availability or payment-confirmation guarantee.

## Owner follow-ups with the most trust value

1. Add clear, current photographs of the toilets beside the cottages and their
   walking route, alongside tent/cottage interiors. Confirm sharing arrangements
   before describing facilities as private. No attached-toilet claim is made.
2. Recheck the review snapshot against the live Google listing. The existing feed
   has no verification date, so its aggregate score is not promoted in the hero.
   Keep review text faithful and let guests see all Google reviews.
3. Confirm that crossed-out regular rates are genuine regular selling prices.
   Remove the comparison if they are no longer accurate. Do not add a discount
   deadline unless it is real and maintained.
4. Give guests a written WhatsApp confirmation with dates, accommodation, guest
   count, agreed total, verified advance, remaining balance and arrival directions.
   Publish response hours only if the team can reliably meet them.
5. Track page views → booking opens → saved requests → WhatsApp handoffs → actual
   team-confirmed bookings. Existing browser events support the first stages;
   use booking records for confirmation. A WhatsApp click is not a sale. Compare
   results over comparable traffic and dates before claiming improvement.
